# ADR 004: Adopt topic taxonomy and a neutral visual system

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-23

## Context

The article taxonomy mixed subjects such as AI with content forms such as
essays and retrospectives. The interface also used several dark surfaces,
three accent colors, gradients, borders, and shadows at the same time. Both
choices weakened information scent and made the site feel like a dashboard
rather than a personal publication.

## Decision

Use five topic categories: AI and intelligent systems, systems and
architecture, IoT and protocols, engineering practice, and learning and
reflection. Keep project domains and article tags as separate dimensions.

Use one neutral grayscale for each theme and a single green semantic accent.
Constrain article and archive widths, use text rows for editorial content, and
reserve cards for projects.

## Consequences

- Every article has one predictable subject category.
- Tags can remain specific without destabilizing navigation.
- Visual hierarchy depends on typography, spacing, and separators instead of
  competing colors or elevation.
- Adding a second accent color now requires a demonstrated semantic need.
